🌻 Happyness

Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Short version: Happyness doesn't ask for your email, phone number, contacts or location. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. You can delete your account and everything you posted at any time, right in the app.

What stays on your device

Your Mood shield settings (muted words, gentle stop, text-only mode), stories you've hidden, and a cache of news headlines are saved only on your device. If you use the app without a community server, your own stories also stay only on your device.

AI moderation

To keep Happyness kind, a new story's text, display name and city may be sent to Anthropic (the provider of the Claude AI model) to check that it follows our guidelines. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and doesn't use it to train its models.

News stories

Headlines come from public news feeds. When the app loads a feed, or you open a story, your device connects to that news website, which can see your IP address under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and stories until you delete them. Deleting your account (Mood shield → Your account → Delete my account and data) immediately removes your account, stories, reactions, reports and blocks from our database. Server logs kept by our hosting provider expire on their normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete any story you wrote, or your whole account, at any time in the app. See also the account deletion page.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or for corrections, by writing to the contact email in the Happyness app store listing.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have further rights, such as to object or to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Happyness isn't directed at children under 13, and you must be at least 13 (or the minimum age in your country) to post.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. We store only a hash of your account key. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't post private information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app. Questions? Write to the contact email in the Happyness app store listing.
