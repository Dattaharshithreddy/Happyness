🌻 Happyness

Community guidelines & terms

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Happyness is a calm corner of the internet: only kind, happy stories. Many people come here to rest from content that makes them anxious or sad, so please help keep it gentle.

Please share

  • Small joys: a kind stranger, a proud moment, a funny pet, a lovely day
  • Your own experiences, in your own words

Not allowed

  • Sad, distressing or upsetting content, including grief, illness and self-harm
  • Anything unkind, mocking, bullying, hateful or discriminatory
  • Sexual or suggestive content, nudity, or anything not suitable for teenagers
  • Violence, crime, weapons, drugs or dangerous activities
  • Politics, elections or divisive news
  • Spam, advertising, links, scams, or pretending to be someone else
  • Other people's private information (full names with locations, addresses, phone numbers, photos)
  • Anything illegal, or anything that infringes someone else's rights

How we keep it kind

  • Every new story is checked automatically, and some are held until a person reviews them.
  • Tap ⋯ on any story to report it or block its author. Stories reported by several people are hidden until we review them.
  • We review reports promptly and remove stories that break these guidelines. Accounts that break them seriously or repeatedly are banned.

If you're having a hard time

Happyness is not a support or medical service, and we can't respond to posts about difficult feelings. If you're struggling, please talk to someone you trust or contact a local helpline. If you're in danger, call your local emergency number. 💛

Terms of use

  • You must be at least 13 (or the minimum age in your country) to post.
  • You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you allow Happyness to store and show it to other users, and to remove it.
  • News headlines link to their original publishers, which own that content.
  • Happyness is provided as-is, without warranties. We may change or stop the service, or remove content or accounts that break these rules.
  • You can stop using Happyness and delete your account at any time (Mood shield → Your account).

See also our privacy policy. Questions or a problem to report? Write to the contact email in the Happyness app store listing.
