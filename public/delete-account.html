Delete your Happyness account

Happyness

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Happyness and tap the 🛡️ shield (top right).
  2. Scroll to Your account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account, all your stories, your reactions, reports and blocks from our servers. Settings saved on your device are cleared when you uninstall the app.

Can't open the app any more?

Happyness accounts aren't linked to an email or phone number, so we can't look you up by name. Email the contact email in the Happyness app store listing with the exact text of the stories you posted (and the display name you used), and we'll delete them and the account they belong to within 30 days.

What's deleted and what's kept

  • Deleted: account ID, display name, stories, reactions, reports and blocks.
  • Kept: nothing in our database. Standard server logs held by our hosting provider expire on their normal schedule.
